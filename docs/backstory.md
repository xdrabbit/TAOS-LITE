# Backstory — held for a future "Our story" page

This file exists to preserve, verbatim, the original v1 `/about` dedication.

It was the whole of `/about` from the first release until 2026-08-19, when
`/about` was rewritten as a professional product page: TAOS is now handed to
strangers by QR code, and a signed personal dedication is not what a stranger
scanning a code at a table should land on. **The dedication was not cut for
being wrong. It was moved for being private.** It is kept here as source
material for a future "Our story" page, where it belongs to the people who
choose to read it rather than to everyone who scans the code.

Nothing in this file is rendered by the app. Do not reword it — it is signed,
authored prose. See `ENHANCEMENTS.md` → Shipped for the decision trail.

---

## The original v1 dedication (verbatim)

**Page title:** About TAOS · Made for Lizmariett Marquez

**Meta description:** TAOS was built for one person — Lizmariett Marquez — so
that love wouldn't be lost in translation.

**Heading:** About TAOS · Acerca de TAOS

> TAOS was built for one person: **Lizmariett Marquez**.
>
> It started because two people who loved each other didn't share a language —
> and we refused to let that be the distance between us. Every piece of this
> exists so that a "good morning" or an "I love you" could land exactly the way
> it was meant to. Liz is the heart of it: the reason it exists, the first voice
> it learned, and the one who believed in it before it was anything at all.

---

> TAOS nació para una persona: **Lizmariett Marquez**.
>
> Empezó porque dos personas que se amaban no hablaban el mismo idioma — y nos
> negamos a dejar que eso fuera la distancia entre nosotros. Cada parte de esta
> app existe para que una conversación fuera posible, para que un "buenos días"
> o un "te amo" llegara tal como se quería decir. Liz es el corazón de todo
> esto: la razón por la que existe, la primera voz que aprendió, y la que creyó
> en ella antes de que fuera algo.

> *Para Liz y su familia en Venezuela — con todo el cariño.*
>
> — Tom

**Footer line:** Made for Lizmariett Marquez ❤️
